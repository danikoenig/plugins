    Install

    No Terminal needed. The first time takes one extra click.

    Want everything?All 10 installers, uninstallers and manuals in one ZIP (about 50 MB).
    Download all 10 (ZIP)
    1. Download the installer

      Click Download on the plugin you want. You get a file like OneShooter-Installer.pkg.

    2. Double-click it

      macOS says it can't check the file. That's normal for free plugins not sold through Apple. Click Done, not Move to Trash.

    3. Allow it once

      System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down, click Open Anyway and enter your Mac password.

    4. Install and rescan

      Click Continue and Install. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins. VST3, AU and CLAP are installed together.

    ComputerMac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Intel Macs are not supported.

    Uninstall

    Every plugin has its own uninstaller next to the download button. Download it, double-click it and allow it the same way as the installer.
